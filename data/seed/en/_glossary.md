Terms fixed for the EN translation of `data/seed/en/`. Any future translation or editing should follow this table.
Status of all content: `machine_reviewed_pending_client`.

## Style rules

- US English, the marketing tone of GetYourGuide/Viator: second person ("you'll"), short sentences, no literal calques ("análisis técnico" → "guided visit", "jornada" → "day").
- Titles in Title Case; durations as `4 Days / 3 Nights` (drop the leading zero: "04 días" → "4 Days"). `durationLabel` (`4D/3N`) is not changed.
- Times in 12-hour format: `4:30 a.m.`, `1:00 p.m.` ("13:00 hrs" → "1:00 p.m."; "12:00 p.m." = noon).
- Altitude: always `3,399 m (11,150 ft)` the first time it appears in each field (feet rounded to the nearest 10). Depths, distances, heights of structures and weights stay metric only (`3,000 m deep`, `9 m tall`, `12 km`, `5 kg`).
- Temperatures: `28°C (82°F)`.
- Currency: `S/.` / `S/` and `USD` exactly as in the source; amounts are never converted.
- Closing line of the itinerary: `——- END OF OUR SERVICES ——-`.
- Emojis from the source (🛏️, 🔹, 📲…) are kept.
- An explanation in parentheses the first time a local term appears (e.g. "totora (reed)", "soroche (altitude sickness)").
