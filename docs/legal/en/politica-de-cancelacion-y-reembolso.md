# Booking, Payment and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Version 2.0 (PWT-LEG-001)

We are committed to providing safe, responsible and high-quality travel experiences, with clear and transparent information throughout the booking process.

The following policies apply to tours, excursions, travel packages and services offered by PERU WONDERS TOURS.

## 1. Bookings and confirmations
All bookings are subject to availability.

To make a reservation, customers must provide the required information and complete the applicable payment.

A reservation is confirmed once availability has been verified, the required payment has been received and an official booking confirmation has been sent by email.

The confirmation will include the booked services, travel dates, amount paid, outstanding balance and applicable conditions.

If a reservation cannot be confirmed, customers will be offered alternatives or a full refund.

## 2. Payment terms
A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking.

The remaining balance must be paid no later than 15 calendar days before the trip begins.

Bookings made less than 15 days before departure require full payment.

Payments may be made in US dollars (USD) or Peruvian soles (PEN) through our available payment methods.

Prices include applicable taxes and mandatory charges. Any applicable exchange rate will be disclosed before payment.

Certain services may require special payment conditions, which will be communicated before booking.

## 3. Outstanding balance
If the remaining balance is not paid by the due date, PERU WONDERS TOURS will contact the customer to arrange payment.

If payment remains outstanding after notification and the additional payment period provided, the booking may be cancelled under the applicable cancellation policy.

## 4. Customer cancellations
Cancellation requests may be submitted by email, WhatsApp or through the official channels available for booking.

Cancellation notice periods are calculated in calendar days, based on the date the request is received in Peru local time (UTC-5).

The following maximum cancellation charges are calculated on the total price of the booked service:

| Notice before departure | Maximum cancellation charge |
| --- | --- |
| 31 days or more | 10% |
| 16–30 days | 25% |
| 8–15 days | 50% |
| 3–7 days | 75% |
| 2 days or less | Up to 100% |
| No-show | Up to 100% |

Cancellation charges must be reasonable, proportionate and supported by actual costs and commitments incurred. Charges under this general policy will not exceed the amount already paid.

## 5. Services subject to special conditions
Certain services may be subject to specific cancellation, modification and refund restrictions, including:

- Machu Picchu admission tickets.
- Inca Trail permits.
- Train tickets.
- Airline tickets.
- Special-rate hotel reservations.
- Peak-season services.
- Other advance-purchase services.

Any special restrictions and potentially non-recoverable costs will be disclosed and accepted before payment.

Charges will not be duplicated, and statutory consumer rights will remain unaffected.

## 6. Booking changes and rescheduling
Customers may request changes to travel dates, itineraries or booked services.

Changes are subject to availability, supplier conditions and previously disclosed price differences.

No additional charges will be applied without the customer's agreement.

## 7. Cancellations by PERU WONDERS TOURS
If PERU WONDERS TOURS is unable to provide a booked service, customers will be offered the appropriate alternatives:

- Rescheduling without a cancellation penalty.
- An equivalent alternative service, subject to customer acceptance.
- A full refund when the entire booked service is cancelled.

For partially undelivered services, customers will receive the applicable refund for services not provided and retain any other applicable legal rights.

## 8. Force majeure and extraordinary circumstances
In the event of extreme weather, natural disasters, strikes, demonstrations, government restrictions, attraction closures or other extraordinary circumstances preventing delivery of the booked services, PERU WONDERS TOURS will promptly inform affected customers.

Rescheduling or applicable refunds for undelivered services will be offered in accordance with applicable law.

The safety and well-being of our travelers remain our priority.

## 9. Refund procedure
Once the refundable amount has been determined, customers will receive an explanation of the calculation and applicable charges.

Refunds will be processed within 15 business days of that determination, unless a shorter statutory deadline applies.

Whenever possible, refunds will be issued using the original payment method and currency.

Bank and payment provider processing times may vary.

## 10. No-show and voluntary withdrawal
If a customer fails to arrive at the agreed time and location or voluntarily leaves a tour after it has started, applicable cancellation conditions and justified non-recoverable expenses may apply.

This does not restrict customer rights in cases of provider non-performance.

## 11. Travel documentation
Customers must provide accurate information and hold valid travel documents, including passports, identification, visas and other applicable requirements.

PERU WONDERS TOURS will provide information regarding specific requirements for the booked services.

## 12. Travel insurance
We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering medical expenses, accidents, trip cancellation, trip interruption and emergency evacuation, particularly for adventure activities and high-altitude destinations.

These policies are interpreted in accordance with Peruvian law and do not waive any statutory consumer rights.
